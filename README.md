## 🌐 Overview / Gambaran Umum

**EPISTEME** (*derived from classical Greek ἐπιστήμη — the philosophical pursuit of principled, verifiable scientific knowledge*) is a cross-disciplinary, zero-dependency autonomous academic research engine.

Designed for scholars, engineers, and graduate researchers across all fields, EPISTEME automates and visualizes the essential phases of empirical scientific inquiry:
1. **Agent 01: Literature Scout (`Dr. Aris`)** — Explores academic repositories, curates seminal publications, and generates core research questions.
2. **Agent 02: Research Methodologist (`Elena`)** — Translates theoretical gaps into rigorous factorial experimental protocols and telemetry measurement schemas.
3. **Agent 03: Senior Peer Reviewer (`Prof. Vance`)** — Conducts critical validity audits, assesses methodological threats, verifies ethical compliance, and issues official publication clearance.

All operations take place within an interactive **1980s Academic Research Laboratory** rendered on HTML5 Canvas 2D, backed by real-time audio synthesis (Web Audio API), synchronized local-time celestial physics, and an interactive resident cat (*Pixel*).

> **📌 Research Paper Limitation & Universal Scope Note:**  
> The EPISTEME framework is structurally **domain-agnostic and universal**—applicable to medicine, physics, social sciences, engineering, and humanities. For empirical demonstration and paper validation purposes, the accompanying case study focuses on *Educational Technology & Human-Computer Interaction (AIED / HCI)* as an illustrative testbed (methodological limitation).

#### Detailed Agent Specifications:

1. **Station 01: Literature Scout (`Dr. Aris`)**
   - **Hardware Artifacts**: Deluxe 1980s IBM PC 5150 workstation, dual 5.25" floppy disk drives with animated read/write LEDs, green phosphor CRT display with scanlines, and an authentic ceramic IEEE mug.
   - **Analytical Capabilities**:
     - *AI Seminal Literature Curation*: When your personal API key (e.g., OpenAI GPT-4o-mini / Gemini) is connected, Dr. Aris queries the LLM to curate the top 3 seminal papers with concrete rationales for why each paper is essential.
     - *OpenAlex Live API Integration*: When run without an API key, connects directly to [OpenAlex](https://openalex.org) (250M+ open catalog), applying dynamic corpus sampling across pages 1–3 and Fisher-Yates shuffling so results are fresh on every run.
     - *SLR Search Triads*: Exports tailored Boolean search query strings for ACM Digital Library, IEEE Xplore, and ERIC/Scopus.

2. **Station 02: Research Methodologist (`Elena`)**
   - **Hardware Artifacts**: Tilted wooden drafting table, architectural blueprint schematics, clear acrylic 45° triangle ruler, drafting stool, and a wall-mounted investigative corkboard with red string matrix.
   - **Analytical Capabilities**:
     - *Experimental Design*: Between-subjects or mixed factorial experimental protocols ($N=128$, randomized double-blind stratification).
     - *Variable Operationalization*: Independent Variables (IV), Dependent Variables (DV with mathematical formulas), and Controlled Variables (CV).
     - *Telemetry Specification*: Micro-behavioral telemetry logs (Inter-Key Intervals, dwell time, prompt revision distance).
     - *Statistical Verification*: Repeated-measures ANOVA, Intraclass Correlation Coefficients (ICC > 0.82), and effect size targets.

3. **Station 03: Senior Peer Reviewer (`Prof. Vance`)**
   - **Hardware Artifacts**: Executive walnut desk with green leather blotter, classic brass & emerald banker’s lamp with warm radial glow, English porcelain teacup with saucer, bound dissertation volume, and heavy brass validation stamp.
   - **Analytical Capabilities**:
     - *Threats to Validity*: Identifies subtle risks across internal validity (cognitive fatigue), external validity (cohort generalizability), and construct validity (AI sycophancy).
     - *Ethical & Regulatory Compliance*: Verification of FERPA, GDPR Article 22, and SHA-256 client telemetry anonymization.
     - *Official Publication Recommendation*: Formal acceptance recommendations targeted at tier-1 international journals with animated brass stamp impact.

### 🎨 1980s Academic Office & Canvas Features

* **Synchronized 24-Hour Celestial Campus Window**:
  - Automatically calculates user local wall-clock time (`new Date()`).
  - Features 5 distinct day/night visual cycles:
    - **Night (20:30–05:00)**: Twinkling celestial stars, glowing crescent moon with lunar haze, Gothic campus spires, lit dormitory windows, and illuminated Victorian streetlamps.
    - **Dawn (05:00–06:45)**: Pastel blush, coral, and apricot morning sunrise with the sun rising over the university horizon.
    - **Daytime (06:45–17:00)**: Azure blue sky, drifting cumulus clouds, red-brick halls, and a soft translucent sunbeam streaming onto the laboratory floor.
    - **Sunset (17:00–19:15)**: Brilliant violet and fiery amber-orange golden hour.
    - **Dusk (19:15–20:30)**: Velvet twilight and emerging evening stars.
* **Synchronized Institutional Wall Clock**:
  - Classic dark-cherry wooden institutional wall clock mounted above the break nook ($x: 350, y: 52$).
  - Continuous, real-time hour, minute, and crimson sweep second hands synchronized with your computer clock.
* **Lab Resident Cat — "Pixel" (🐾)**:
  - **Night Mode**: Curled asleep inside the handcrafted wooden arched sanctuary (*kandang*) on a tartan cushion, with rhythmic breathing and floating `Zzz` particles.
  - **Day Mode**: Actively prowls across the lab with an authentic 4-legged feline walk cycle, laps milk at her ceramic dish (with animated ripple rings), grooms fur, stretches, and watches Dr. Aris CRT monitor.
  - **Interactive**: Click or tap Pixel anytime to hear a retro FM synthesis meow/purr and spawn floating hearts (`❤️`).
* **Academic Break Nook & Vintage Toaster Oven**:
  - Wooden credenza hosting a 1980s drip coffee maker with rising steam and an enamel toaster oven with glowing coils and croissants.
  - Click to toggle the toaster oven door and trigger an authentic toaster bell chime (`ping!`).
* **Zero-Dependency Sound Synthesizer (Web Audio API)**:
  - Generates procedural retro mechanical clicks, corkboard pushpin thuds, brass stamp impacts, data handoff arpeggios, and cat meows without external MP3 files.

## 🇮🇩 Panduan Bahasa Indonesia

### 🌟 Gambaran & Filosofi Sistem

**EPISTEME** (*diambil dari bahasa Yunani kuno ἐπιστήμη — pengetahuan ilmiah sejati berbasis prinsip metodologis*) adalah platform riset multi-agen otonom tanpa ketergantungan pustaka luar (*zero-dependency*). Platform ini dirancang untuk dapat digunakan oleh akademisi, dosen, dan peneliti di **semua bidang keilmuan** (lintas disiplin).

### 🤖 Tiga Agen Riset Utama
1. **Stasiun 1: Literature Scout (Dr. Aris)**
   * Menggali literatur ilmiah riil dari basis data global OpenAlex atau langsung dikurasi oleh model AI pribadi Anda (OpenAI GPT-4o / Gemini).
   * Menghasilkan 3 Rumusan Masalah Utama (*Research Questions*) dan kueri Boolean terstruktur untuk ACM DL, IEEE Xplore, dan Scopus/ERIC.
2. **Stasiun 2: Research Methodologist (Elena)**
   * Merancang metodologi eksperimen faktorial $2 	imes 2$ ($N=128$, alokasi acak tersamar ganda).
   * Menyusun matriks variabel (Bebas, Terikat, dan Kontrol) serta skema telemetri interaksi pengguna (latensi pengetikan, jeda baca).
3. **Stasiun 3: Senior Peer Reviewer (Prof. Vance)**
   * Melakukan audit ancaman validitas (internal, eksternal, konstruk) dan kelaikan etik (GDPR/FERPA, *hashing* SHA-256).
   * Menerbitkan keputusan rekomendasi publikasi berkas resmi dan stempel validasi `[VERIFIED & APPROVED]`.

## ⚡ Quickstart / Cara Menjalankan

EPISTEME dibuat murni menggunakan teknologi standar web terbuka (**HTML5, Canvas 2D, Web Audio API, Vanilla JavaScript**). Tidak memerlukan `npm install`, Node.js server, atau proses build.

### Metode 1: Buka Langsung di Peramban Web (Paling Mudah)
1. Unduh file `index.html`.
2. Klik dua kali file `index.html` untuk membukanya di peramban web modern apa saja (Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, Mozilla Firefox, atau Apple Safari).
3. Aplikasi akan langsung berjalan secara luring (*offline-capable*) maupun daring (*online* untuk penarikan API OpenAlex).

### Metode 2: Menjalankan via Localhost / Live Server (Opsional)
```bash
# Menggunakan Python 3 built-in server
python3 -m http.server 8000

# Buka pada browser:
# http://localhost:8000
```

## ⚙️ BYOK (Bring Your Own Key) Setup

EPISTEME dilengkapi sistem **Universal BYOK (Bring Your Own Key)** yang menjamin privasi Anda: kunci API disimpan secara aman di peramban Anda melalui `localStorage` dan tidak pernah dikirim ke peladen pihak ketiga.

### Penyedia Model yang Didukung:
| Penyedia (*Provider*) | Model Populer | Keunggulan |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Google Gemini** | `gemini-2.5-flash`, `gemini-1.5-flash`, `gemini-1.5-pro` | Cepat, kuota free tier besar |
| **OpenAI** | `gpt-4o-mini`, `gpt-4o`, `o3-mini` | Penalaran ilmiah & kurasi presisi |
| **Groq Cloud** | `llama-3.3-70b-versatile`, `deepseek-r1-distill-llama-70b` | Kecepatan inferensi sub-detik |
| **OpenRouter** | `anthropic/claude-3.5-sonnet`, `deepseek/deepseek-r1` | Akses multi-model universal |
| **Ollama (Local)** | `llama3`, `deepseek-r1`, `qwen2.5` | 100% luring & privat tanpa internet |

### Cara Menghubungkan API Key Anda:
1. Klik tombol **`🔑 MAINFRAME: SETUP`** pada bilah menu atas.
2. Pilih penyedia model Anda (misalnya **OpenAI** atau **Gemini**).
3. Masukkan API Key Anda pada kolom yang tersedia.
4. Klik **"Simpan Konfigurasi / Save Configuration"**.
5. Indikator di pojok kanan atas akan berubah menjadi warna hijau: `[ENGINE: REAL LLM (PROVIDER / MODEL)]`.
6. Ketika Anda memulai riset, model AI Anda akan secara aktif mengkurasi 3 makalah seminal terbaik dan menyusun naskah ilmiah spesifik untuk topik Anda!

## 📚 Real Bibliometrics & OpenAlex Integration

Jika Anda tidak memasukkan API Key pribadi, EPISTEME secara otomatis memanfaatkan katalog data terbuka [OpenAlex API](https://api.openalex.org).
* **Pool Dinamis**: Sistem menarik pool 25–50 artikel ilmiah riil dari halaman acak dan menerapkan algoritma *Fisher-Yates shuffle*.
* **Tombol `[🔄 Ganti Makalah Lain]`**: Mengizinkan pengguna mengganti atau merotasi 3 makalah baru secara instan kapan saja.
* **Tombol `[🎲 Acak Topik]`**: Menyediakan 15+ topik riset mutakhir siap pakai dalam satu klik.
* **Tautan DOI Langsung**: Setiap entri makalah menyertakan tautan DOI aktif menuju penerbit resmi (Springer, IEEE, ACM, Nature, Elsevier).
